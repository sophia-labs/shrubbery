# Catalogue

Sophia Cluster 1.0, product by product. Everything runs on one server in your own cloud account, and it is yours to keep.

## [Desk](/catalog/desk)

The shared workspace for your company's documents, files and knowledge, where your people and your agents work side by side.

- Documents several people edit at once
- Files up to 50 MB, previewed in the page
- Typed links between any two pages, with backlinks
- Full history, restore points and Trash

*Shipped · next release 1.0.1*

## [Compute](/catalog/compute)

The agents you hire, and the equipment they use: an assistant in every workspace, custom agents in isolated sandboxes, and any model you choose.

- An assistant that reads, or reads and edits, with a record of every change
- Your choice of model, with the price of every reply shown
- Custom agents that run on a schedule
- Your own model key on your own install

*Shipped · next release 1.0.1*

## [Chat](/catalog/chat)

Team chat that lives beside your workspace, so what gets decided in conversation can join the record.

- Built on Zulip, the open-source team chat
- Runs on your own Cluster, like everything else

*In progress*

## [Platform](/catalog/platform)

The equipment itself: one server in your own cloud account, installed by us, with standard interfaces and your data in open formats.

- Installed in your AWS account, at your own address
- Hourly backups to your own storage, with a weekly restore test
- Connect your own AI tools over MCP, the open standard
- Export everything in open formats, any time

*Shipped · next release 1.0.1*
